# HomePDF icon design

White H in Segoe UI Black, centered on its ink bounds, at 66% icon height. The background is the SnagItOpen rounded-square design: 3% inset, 22% corner radius, top-to-bottom gradient, dark border, soft glyph shadow above 32 px. Green palette: #166534 to #064E3B; border #043F2E; shadow #002C18 at the reference opacity. Frames: 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 256 px. Preserve classic 32-bit DIB frames below 256 and PNG at 256.
